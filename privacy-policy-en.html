Aiya Ledger Privacy Policy Updated and effective: 24 October 2026 Applies to: Aiya Ledger 1.7.0 for iOS Publisher: duhaley, individual developer Privacy and support: 924323178@qq.com Aiya Ledger is a personal bookkeeping app that does not require an app account. This policy explains how the app handles data, the choices you have and how to contact us. 1. Local records and statement imports Account names, currencies, balances, income, expenses, custom categories and tags, dates, notes, budgets, templates, recurring bills, classification rules, savings goals and movements, and preferences stay on your device for bookkeeping, reports and reminders. The publisher does not operate a ledger server, and the app does not automatically send your ledger to the publisher. CSV / XLSX statements you select are parsed on your device. Import sources, transaction references, merchants, payment methods and file fingerprints support duplicate review and provenance and are included in complete JSON backups. The app does not automatically upload statements to Alipay, WeChat or banks, request bank or payment-platform passwords, read SMS messages or contacts, or fetch statements from other apps. 2. Backups and sharing you choose You can manually export complete JSON backups or CSV files, or save and restore backups through your own iCloud Drive or HTTPS WebDAV service. You initiate these actions. Complete backups include amounts, accounts, notes, budgets, recurring bills, templates, preferences and import provenance even when amounts are hidden on screen. There are no scheduled background backups or real-time ledger merging. WebDAV addresses, usernames and passwords are used only in the current backup-screen session and sent to the HTTPS service you specify; they are not written into the ledger or backups. Apple and your Apple account settings manage iCloud. The publisher does not receive your WebDAV credentials and cannot use the app to access ledgers in your private cloud storage. Cloud and sharing services may retain files, IP addresses, access times or request metadata under their own policies. Use services you trust and review their policies. iCloud uploads and downloads may take time to complete. 3. Pro purchases and restoration Aiya Pro is an optional, non-consumable one-time Apple In-App Purchase with no automatic renewal. Apple StoreKit handles purchases, restoration and transaction verification. The app determines access from system-verified transactions, including revocations or refunds. Purchasing does not upload your ledger to Apple. The app does not read or store Apple account passwords, payment card numbers or banking payment credentials. Purchase credentials and Pro access are not stored in ledger backups. Restoring a ledger does not grant Pro. Previous purchases can be restored in Settings > Aiya Pro. Apple processes store accounts, transactions and payments under its own policies. 4. Optional local notifications Recurring-bill reminders are off by default. The app requests iOS notification permission only when you enable them. Scheduling happens on your device. Notifications show a general confirmation reminder without amounts, account names or bill names and do not send bill data to the publisher. The reminder preference is device-specific and is not included in ledger backups. You can turn reminders off in the app or revoke notification permission in system settings. You can view bills without notification permission. 5. Storage, security and deletion The ledger is saved as local preferences in JSON. Ledger and exported JSON / CSV files are not encrypted by the app. Hiding amounts only masks the screen and is not an app lock. HTTPS protects WebDAV transport but does not provide end-to-end file encryption. Protect your device, keep regular backups and store exported files safely. You can edit or delete records or clear the local ledger in the app. A validated backup replaces the current ledger only after confirmation. Deleting local data or uninstalling does not remove cloud backups, exports, recipient copies or system backups; manage those in the relevant service. Uninstalling or device failure may remove unbacked-up records. Apple and device settings govern OS backups and migration. There is no publisher-operated app account to close. You can manage local data directly on your device without asking the publisher to delete data the publisher never received. 6. Advertising, tracking and support The app has no ads and integrates no advertising tracking, behavioral analytics or developer crash-reporting service. We do not sell your personal information. The app does not request location, contacts, microphone or camera permissions. System interfaces and services you select handle file selection and sharing. If you email support, we receive your sender address and the content you choose to provide, solely to reply, troubleshoot or meet necessary obligations. Do not send complete ledgers, passwords or full bank card details. We retain support data only as needed to address the matter and remove it when no longer necessary, subject to legal obligations. You can contact the support email to request access, correction or deletion of support data you provided. The publisher will choose a website to host this policy. Website logs, cookies and hosting services should be described by that website. Website data handling is separate from the app and does not mean the app automatically uploads your ledger. 7. Children and changes The app is not specifically directed to children and does not deliberately collect child identity information. Minors should manage devices, backups and purchases with a guardian's guidance. We will update this policy and its date when features or data practices change, with notices in the app or policy page where appropriate. Contact 924323178@qq.com for privacy questions. 8. Apple Watch companion When you install and open the companion, the paired iPhone and Apple Watch use system Watch Connectivity to exchange monthly income, expense and budget summaries, account, category and tag options, spending-review and savings-goal summaries for unlocked Pro users, theme, language and hidden-amount preferences. Newly sent summaries omit amounts when hiding is enabled. Entries you confirm on the watch stay locally pending until the phone validates and saves them; connectivity, closed apps or changed accounts may delay delivery. Random request IDs are stored and backed up with the ledger to prevent duplicate entries, not to track identity. Processed IDs survive local clearing or restoration so delayed messages cannot recreate entries. Uninstalling may remove unsynced entries; sync first. This does not send data to the publisher, make real payments, or provide cloud backup or purchasing on the watch. 9. Home Screen widgets Widgets use an on-device App Group shared only by this app and its widget extension. The shared snapshot contains the displayed currency, theme, language, update time, monthly budget and daily income/expense totals, without account names, notes or full entries. When hiding amounts is enabled, newly published snapshots omit all amounts. WidgetKit controls refresh timing, so a previous snapshot may remain visible briefly. Open the app to request a refresh. Widget links open the calendar or entry editor; they do not save entries automatically. No widget data is sent to the developer. 10. Multiple ledgers Ledger names and each ledger's accounts, entries and plans stay on your device. Switching does not merge them. Theme, language and amount privacy are shared. Multi-ledger JSON/cloud backups contain all ledgers; CSV exports only the current ledger. Restoring a multi-ledger backup replaces all ledgers after confirmation; a legacy single-ledger backup replaces only the current ledger. Paired-watch and on-device widget snapshots include a ledger identifier to keep records separate and may display the name when amounts are visible. New hidden-amount snapshots omit that name. Pending watch entries retain their original ledger identifier after the phone switches. These records are not sent to the publisher. 11. Receipts, assets and settlements JPG, PNG and PDF receipts you choose are stored locally with entries and included in complete JSON and cloud backups, but not CSV. Each entry supports three files up to 1 MiB each. No OCR or publisher upload occurs. Asset/debt flags and refund/reimbursement links are processed locally. The paired watch may receive net assets, liabilities and pending claim totals in the current currency, without receipt files. Monthly and yearly image cards are previewed and shared only at your request; check selected amounts and destinations. Deleting local receipts does not delete earlier exports, backups or shared copies.
